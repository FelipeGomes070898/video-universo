A light-year is 9.46 trillion kilometers — a number your brain can't feel. So we shrink the Solar System until the Sun is a football in São Paulo's Praça da Sé, and then walk one light-year across a real 3D map of Brazil, from São Paulo to Salvador.

Voyager 1 is about to reach one light-day from Earth after 49 years of flight. On this scale it has traveled only 4 kilometers. How long would one light-year take by plane, by car, on foot, or at Voyager's speed? Tell me in the comments: which comparison surprised you most?
